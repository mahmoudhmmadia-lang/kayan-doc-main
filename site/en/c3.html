Human Resources

3.1

What you will achieve

Result: know who works, their working time, and how to review leave and pay. Start with Sarah, then repeat for the team.

3.2

HR pages at a glance

Start with people and structure, then time, then payroll if used. This is priority order; prepare units and locations before linking positions. Sections vary by setup and permissions; search by page name.

Open: HR / listed section / page. Setup walkthrough: Section 2.6.

Experience levels and holiday dates are inside their groups, not extra app pages.

Read from the assignment to the records it needs. Sarah is the member; HR Manager is the position. Unit and location links are optional. Groups do not replace these links.

Dashed arrows are review steps, not automatic processing. Skip payroll if you only need to organize the team.

3.3

Check the assignment

Open HR / Workforce / Assignments. Check Sarah’s member, position and start date. If missing, follow Section 2.6. Keep the same member record when her job changes.

3.4

Create a shift

A shift sets expected time; attendance shows what happened. Owner: HR.

  1. 1
    HR / Time / Shifts: add “Office Hours”, code HZN-S-01, type “Regular”.
  2. 2
    Choose Sunday–Thursday and Asia/Damascus. Name, code, type and at least one workday are Required.
  3. 3
    Work Hours: 09:00–17:00. Edit the existing break to 13:00–13:30; do not add a duplicate. Breaks are Optional.
  4. 4
    Check Total Hours = 07:30, then “Save”. Reopen and check the values.

The image demonstrates hours; use the code and timezone in the steps above. For split shifts, add each period. For flexible/marathon shifts, enter hours and minutes.

Avoid time errors

Periods must not overlap; breaks stay inside work time. 22:00–06:00 ends the next day. Equal start/end does not mean a full day.

When importing, check the preview; template and form fields differ by type.

3.5

How the shift reaches the employee

Inheritance: use a shift from a higher record instead of repeating it.

Stop at the first shift found. Unit and parents precede location and parents; shifts are not combined.

  1. 1
    Open HR / Core / Nodes / HZN-HR.
  2. 2
    Select “Office Hours” in “Shift”. Use “Override” if needed to choose it, then save.
  3. 3
    Open Workforce / Positions / HR Manager. Check unit and shift.
  4. 4
    Open Sarah’s assignment and check the shift. If different, check assignment then position as shown.

“Reset” restores inheritance. After changing a unit’s shift, check assignments with overrides.

3.6

Review an attendance day

You need an assignment, shift and recorded attendance. Creating an employee does not create past attendance.

  1. 1
    Open HR / Time / My attendance, then month and day. Viewing another member requires permission.
  2. 2
    Compare shift, clocking times and duration. For a period or several employees, open “Timesheets” and set people and dates.
  3. 3
    For a discrepancy, check the source with the attendance administrator. Open “Attendance Corrections” for an available correction; check member, date and decision.

Check: you can explain the difference. If empty, check member, period, permission and source; an empty screen does not prove absence.

Shift and policy

The shift sets expected time; policy handles lateness and overtime. Review the result; do not assume automatic deductions or pay.

3.7

Holidays and leave

A holiday is shared; leave belongs to an employee. A leave type is a reusable rule; a leave record applies it on a date.

This is a workflow, not automatic approval. Owner: HR.

  1. 1
    HR / Time / Leave Types: search first. If a new type is needed and permitted, add “Annual (paid)”, choose payment treatment and the percentage for partial pay. Save and check.
  2. 2
    Check that the type is available in the employee’s job settings; its existence does not grant entitlement to everyone.
  3. 3
    After approval: Time / Leaves / add. Select Sarah, type, start/end times and the authorized status. Duration is calculated. Save.
  4. 4
    Reopen and check person, type, date and duration. Review the period in attendance and pay.

Shared holidays: Time / Holiday Groups / group. Add name, date and full day, partial day or period. Enter partial-day hours or period start/end; save and check links to units or jobs.

If the effect is missing: check date, type, links and status. Submitting a request does not mean leave is approved and recorded.

3.8

Prepare pay data

Owner: payroll staff after time review. The salary scale is a reference; assignment data is still needed.

In Salary Scale, save the experience group, levels, increase rule and currency, then seniority levels and values. Open the matrix and check intersections. Arrows show setup order, not automatic payment.

  1. 1
    Check assignment period, pay, currency, time, leave and corrections.
  2. 2
    HR / Payment / Payroll Inputs: search first to avoid duplicates.
  3. 3
    To add: complete * fields, the period and approved amount. Save and open “Preview calculation”. Correct discrepancies, then complete authorized approval.

Check: amount and calculation method are correct. Preview is not payment.

3.9

Slip, then payroll

Review Sarah’s slip first. Use your organization’s approved pay and currency.

Salary slip:

  1. 1
    HR / Payment / Salary Slips / “Create salary slips”.
  2. 2
    Choose “Period end”, then Sarah’s row and position. Check “Period start”; the first slip defaults to assignment start and can be changed.
  3. 3
    Create drafts using the button showing the selected count; open her slip. If a draft exists, review it instead of duplicating.
  4. 4
    Choose “Calculate”. Check period, earnings, deductions and net pay. Use “Recalculate” after changing inputs.
  5. 5
    When items are correct, choose “Approve” as permitted. Read and confirm the prompt; check the list status.

Payroll:

  1. 1
    Payment / Payrolls / “Generate payroll”.
  2. 2
    Set month, year, filters and employees; payroll uses approved slips in range.
  3. 3
    Choose “Generate”. Resolve conflicts with existing payroll. Open the result; check people, period, total and status.

Check: trace each amount to its slip and items. If someone is missing, check period, filters, assignment and slip status.

Calculation is not payment

Check payment status and records under your organization’s procedure. Creating payroll does not prove receipt of money.
