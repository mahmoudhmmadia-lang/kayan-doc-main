7.5

The tray and the letter lifecycle

The “Correspondence tray” groups available work into folders. A folder helps you find work, while a status describes the letter’s stage. “Awaiting my approval” is a personal work view, and issuance does not by itself prove delivery or completion.

Example: Horizon

Sarah opens “Inbox” at ① to find a letter about the Aleppo branch visit, then searches for its subject at ②. She uses “Filters” at ③ if she needs to narrow the results and reloads with ④ before checking the result.

  1. 1
    Open “Correspondence tray”, then select “Inbox”, “Drafts” or “Awaiting my approval” for your work.
  2. 2
    Search for a word from a known letter’s subject and inspect the results. You can use “Filters” or quick filters such as “Unread” and “Urgent”.
  3. 3
    Clear the search and filter if the letter is missing, then check the capacity and folder. Use “Refresh” to reload the list.
  4. 4
    Open an available letter and read its subject, status, priority and confidentiality before taking action.

Practice and check: Switch from “Inbox” to “Drafts” and back, then search and clear the search. Confirm the folder changes and the list returns. If it is empty, record that observation rather than concluding the organization has no letters. If “The folder could not be loaded” appears, use “Try again”; a loading error is not an empty search result.
