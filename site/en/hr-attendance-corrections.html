Attendance Corrections

3.15

Attendance Corrections

This page gathers days that need fixing, such as a missing punch, so you can correct their punches and approve or reject the decision.

When is it useful? To handle the team's wrong days before payroll, instead of hunting for them day by day. Open: HR / Time / Attendance Corrections.

Example: Horizon

At Horizon, Omar forgot to clock out. He requests a correction with the right time, then checks his day after the decision.

What can you do?

  • Add a punch, change its time or direction, or remove it. For a day with no punches: “Register leave”.
  • “Save correction”, “Send for approval”, “Approve”, “Reject”; depending on your role.
  • Dismiss a flag: “Confirm absence” or “Not an issue”. For bulk dismiss, type the number to confirm.
  • The correction deadline is set at the bottom of the Attendance policy page.

Try it and check

  1. 1
    Open Attendance Corrections, choose “Needs action” and the HR unit.
  2. 2
    Open the day, add the missing punch, choose the reason, then “Send for approval”.

Check: the day moves to “Awaiting approval”, then “Resolved” after the decision. If the action is unavailable, check the deadline and permissions.
