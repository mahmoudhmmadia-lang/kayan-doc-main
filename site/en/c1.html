Start with KayanOS

1.1

What is KayanOS?

KayanOS brings employees, tasks, requests and files into one workspace. See who owns the work and its status, within your permissions.

Define responsibilities, enter correct data and keep status up to date.

1.2

Example: Horizon

Our fictional example: Horizon Services Co. in Damascus and Aleppo. Sarah handles HR; Omar coordinates operations.

Before: an office-setup request is lost in chat. Who will do it, and when?

The team performs these steps; automation needs setup.

1.3

Where to start

Start with two employees, two jobs and one shift. Check, then expand.

1.4

First sign-in

You need: the organization address and sign-in method from your administrator. Example: hzn.kayanos.app. To create an organization, see Chapter 2.

  1. 1
    Open the address. Sign in using the email, phone or employee ID prepared for you.
  2. 2
    Check the organization name before entering data.

Check: open a member you can access. Return using the top navigation path. If an app or edit button is missing, ask the administrator to check permissions.

1.5

Names to know

An assignment needs the person and job. Linking the job to a department is optional and recommended.
