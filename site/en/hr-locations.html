3.7

Locations

A location is a work site, such as an office or branch, with its address and map point. It can belong to another location.

When is it useful? It separates place from unit: two units in the Damascus office, or one unit across several sites. Open: HR / Core / Locations.

What can you do?

  • “Map” view with location pins, or the “Tree” of sub-locations.
  • Search by name, code, city or address, and filter by type and country.

Try it and check

  1. 1
    Open Locations and search for Head Office Damascus.
  2. 2
    If needed, add HZN-DAM with name, type, country, state, city and address, set the point on the map, then save. Choose a parent location only if it belongs to one.

Check: open the map view and confirm the pin. Link the location from the position; a location alone does not assign anyone.
