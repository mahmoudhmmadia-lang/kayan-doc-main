\textbf{Limits of the explanation:} the details of the financial calculation and policies depend on the organization's settings.
The explanation of the “Marathon” form is limited to the fields confirmed in the source; no shift rotation
or automatic rest days were inferred from the type's name. The Horizon example is a practice exercise, not proof of existing data.

\textbf{Checked on the test environment} (test2, 2026-10-13): the add-shift form was opened
in Arabic, fictional data was entered, five working days were selected, and the break was changed
with the time controls to 13:00 to 13:30 within working hours of 09:00 to
17:00. The total showed 07:30. A screenshot of the form only was saved, with no personal data.
The record was not saved in the organization. \textbf{Not tested in practice:} saving the shift and its inheritance,
importing it, approving leaves, and generating or paying payroll; their explanation is based on the sources.

\textbf{Additional check for version 0.3:} the “Create salary slips” action was opened on the test
environment, and the appearance of “Period end” and its selection message before members are shown was confirmed.
No slips were created, and calculation, approval and generation were not tested in practice.

\textbf{Template versus form difference:} the shifts template marks work hours and the duration as required,
while the form shows different fields depending on the type. The import preview was not tested for these cases.
