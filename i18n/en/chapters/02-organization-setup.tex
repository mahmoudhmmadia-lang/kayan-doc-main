\section{1. Create the organization}
\label{sec:setup-create}

From sign-in, choose “Create Organization”. The setup administrator does this once.

\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.95\linewidth]{figures/screens/ch02-create-org-en.png}
\caption{The “Create Organization” page: the four steps at the top}
\label{fig:setup-create-screen}
\end{figure}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Contact: enter email or phone.
  \item Verify: enter the 6-digit code within 10 minutes. Request another after one minute.
  \item Admin: enter your name and an optional password.
  \item Organization Details: enter subdomain, name, country and staff range; optionally add a logo. Complete creation.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Check:} sign in at the new address as administrator and confirm the organization name. Keep the address for the team.

\begin{rcnote}[Subdomain]
Use 3–63 lowercase letters, digits or hyphens. No leading/trailing hyphen, existing address or reserved name such as admin.
\end{rcnote}

\begin{rcwarn}[Update or new record?]
An existing code updates its record. A new code creates another record; keep codes when correcting data. Duplicate rows in the file are ignored.
\end{rcwarn}

\subsection{Employee sign-in}
\label{sec:setup-login}

\begin{itemize}
  \item With email or phone: an email/WhatsApp invitation lasts 48 hours. The employee opens it and sets a password of at least 8 characters.
  \item Without email or phone: the administrator sets a password; the employee signs in with their employee ID.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Check:} the employee signs in at the organization address and opens an allowed app. If the invitation is missing, check contact details and the sending channel.

\section{Check the result}
\label{sec:setup-check}

\begin{rcchecklist}[Checklist: setup complete]
\begin{checklist}
  \item The timezone and the default language are set.
  \item Each node is under its correct parent node.
  \item Each position is linked to its node and location.
  \item Each member has at least one assignment.
  \item Each position shows the “Active Members” who hold it.
  \item A test member received the invitation and signed in successfully.
\end{checklist}
\end{rcchecklist}

\section{Problems and fixes}
\label{sec:setup-errors}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Problems and fixes}
\label{tab:setup-errors}
\rcZebra
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{R{4.2cm} Y Y}
\rcHeaderRow
\thh{The problem} & \thh{The cause} & \thh{The fix}\\
Subdomain rejected & In use, reserved, or contains characters that are not allowed. & Choose another name with lowercase letters and numbers.\\
Verification code expired & 10 minutes have passed. & Ask for a new code.\\
Importing assignments failed & The member or position does not exist, or the label is written differently. & Import members and positions first, and copy the label from the system.\\
Duplicate data & A file was imported with new codes for existing records. & Use the same codes so the records are updated.\\
Wrong times & The timezone is still UTC. & Change it in Organization settings.\\
The invitation did not arrive & No email and no phone, or the 48 hours have passed. & Add the email or phone, or set a password for the member.\\
\end{tabularx}
