\chapter{Official Correspondence}
\label{ch:correspondence}

\section{What will you achieve?}

Official correspondence brings the letter, its addressees and its workflow into a record you can follow. This chapter takes you from identifying your capacity and finding your work to preparing, reviewing and approving a letter, then registering, sending, organizing and following it, and ends with app setup and a closing exercise.

\begin{rcnote}[Example: Horizon]
Omar Haddad prepares a request to coordinate the Aleppo branch visit. Sarah Al-Khatib reviews it if assigned to do so. Each checks their capacity first; this fictional training scenario does not describe test records.
\end{rcnote}
