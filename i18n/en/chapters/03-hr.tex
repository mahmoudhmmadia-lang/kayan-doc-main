\section{What you will achieve}

\textbf{Result:} know who works, in which job, when they work, and how to review their leave and pay. Start with Sarah, then repeat for the team.

\textbf{Who does what?} HR: team and leave records. Employees: their attendance. Payroll staff: calculations. Authorized administrators: policies and devices. Tasks and projects use the same people and jobs.

\section{HR pages at a glance}
\label{sec:hr-pages}

Open the pages in this order: people and structure, then time, then payroll if your organization uses it. Each page has its own illustrated explanation. \textbf{Open:} HR / section shown on the card / page.

\textbf{In every list:} table, cards or other views, search, filters and saved views, Excel import and export, and bulk delete, as your permissions allow. Each record page lists what it adds beyond that.
