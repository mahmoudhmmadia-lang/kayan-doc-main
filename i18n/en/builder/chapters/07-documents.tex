\chapter{Documents \& printing}
\label{ch:builder-documents}

\section{What will you build in this chapter?}
\label{sec:builder-documents-intro}

Work often needs an official document produced from a record's data, such as a letter, a statement or a printed form. KayanOS offers three ways to create these documents: Word templates that you write in Word and upload, Excel templates that suit lists and tables, and print templates that you design inside KayanOS without any outside file. The chapter explains each way on short pages: creating the template, then writing its tags or blocks, then testing and publishing it.

Print templates rely on identities and letterheads: the organization's logo, name and official details, and the page header and footer. That is why the chapter ends with two pages about them, one of which covers the letterhead editor. If your documents are official, start with the identity and letterhead, then choose the template type that suits the document.

\begin{rcnote}[Example: Horizon]
Horizon prepares three documents for visit requests: a confirmation letter in Word, a monthly branch visits sheet in Excel, and a visit sheet printed on the company letterhead with a QR code. Sarah Al-Khatib prints the sheet after reviewing request HZN-VR-0001, and the Aleppo Branch checks it is genuine by scanning the code.
\end{rcnote}
