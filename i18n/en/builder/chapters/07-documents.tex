\chapter{Documents \& printing}
\label{ch:builder-documents}

\section{What will you build in this chapter?}
\label{sec:builder-documents-intro}

Work often needs an official document produced from a record's data, such as a letter, a statement or a printed form. This chapter explains three ways to create these documents: Word templates, Excel templates, and print templates that you design inside KayanOS. It also explains the identities and letterheads that give documents the organization's logo and official details.

\begin{rcnote}[Example: Horizon]
Horizon sets up a print template for the visit request with the company letterhead, so Sarah Al-Khatib prints the approved request and sends it to the Aleppo Branch.
\end{rcnote}
