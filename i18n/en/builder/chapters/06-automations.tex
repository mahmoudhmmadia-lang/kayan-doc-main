\section{What will you build in this chapter?}
\label{sec:builder-automations-intro}

This chapter brings together two tools that connect KayanOS with what surrounds it. The “SOPs” page builds automations that connect to external services and accounts, such as email or spreadsheets. The “Bots” page sets up assistants that work in chat, email and calendar, and decides where they are installed and what they may do.

\begin{rcnote}[Example: Horizon]
Horizon sets up a bot called “Visits assistant” that is added to the branches channel, so Omar Haddad can mention it in the conversation when he needs to follow up a visit request.
\end{rcnote}
