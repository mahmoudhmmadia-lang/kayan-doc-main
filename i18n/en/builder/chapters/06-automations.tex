\section{What will you build in this chapter?}
\label{sec:builder-automations-intro}

This chapter brings together two tools that connect KayanOS with what surrounds it. The “SOPs” page opens an embedded automation studio that builds flows starting from a KayanOS event, then reading and checking the data, and finally running an action whose result leaves a reviewable trace. The “Bots” page sets up assistants with a name and picture, decides where members add them in chat, email and calendar, and whether they are installed automatically.

The chapter explains automation on five pages in build order: the overall idea, then triggers, then actions, then connections, then testing and troubleshooting. Two bot pages follow: its capabilities, then its default installation.

\begin{rcnote}[Example: Horizon]
Horizon's system administrator builds an automation that creates a review task for Sarah Al-Khatib whenever Omar Haddad submits a new visit request in “Visit requests”. They also set up the “Visits assistant” bot and add it to the branches channel, so Omar can mention it in the conversation when he needs to follow up his request.
\end{rcnote}
