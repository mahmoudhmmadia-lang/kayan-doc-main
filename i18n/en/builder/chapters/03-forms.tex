\chapter{Forms}
\label{ch:builder-forms}

\section{What will you build in this chapter?}
\label{sec:builder-forms-intro}

A form is a page of questions that an employee or applicant fills in; its answers reach the person who reviews them and may be written into a record type. This chapter explains how to create a form, arrange its pages and questions, link its answers to records, then test it, publish it and follow up what it receives.

\begin{rcnote}[Example: Horizon]
The system administrator creates a “Branch visit request” form; Omar Haddad fills it in, a record is created in “Visit requests” from his answers, and Sarah Al-Khatib reviews it.
\end{rcnote}
