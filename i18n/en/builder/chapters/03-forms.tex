\section{What will you build in this chapter?}
\label{sec:builder-forms-intro}

A form is a page of questions that an employee or applicant fills in; its answers reach the person who reviews them and may be written into a record type. This chapter explains building a form from start to following up its requests: creating it, arranging its pages and questions, setting its rules and buttons, linking it to records, deciding who reaches its responses, then drawing the request journey and its approvals, testing it and publishing it.

Every change you make in the “Form Builder” is saved to a draft, while people always fill the published version. So you can edit and test safely, and no change reaches anyone until you publish.

\begin{rcnote}[Example: Horizon]
The system administrator creates a “Branch visit request” form; Omar Haddad fills it in, and a record is created in “Visit requests” from his answers after Sarah Al-Khatib approves it.
\end{rcnote}
