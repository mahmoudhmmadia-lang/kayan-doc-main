\section{What will you build in this chapter?}
\label{sec:builder-internal-sops-intro}

An internal SOP is a workflow of consecutive steps that starts on an event you choose, such as a record being created or a form being submitted. Its steps may request an approval, create a task or send a notification, so getting the work done does not depend on someone remembering the next step. This chapter explains the whole tool: the home page, the editor, triggers and step types, setting up each step and handling its errors, then testing, publishing, following runs and responding to approvals.

Your edits are saved in a draft that does not run until you publish it, so every SOP goes through the same cycle: build it, test it, publish it, then follow its runs.

\begin{rcnote}[Example: Horizon]
When a new visit request is created, an internal SOP requests Sarah Al-Khatib's approval, and once she approves it creates the visit preparation task for Omar Haddad.
\end{rcnote}
