\chapter{Services}
\label{ch:builder-services}

\section{What will you build in this chapter?}
\label{sec:builder-services-intro}

A service offers one or more forms to the public or to employees under a clear name, with its conditions, fees and a known path. This chapter explains how to set up a service and publish it on the portal, then receive its requests and follow up its performance.

\begin{rcnote}[Example: Horizon]
Horizon offers “Branch visit request” as an internal service for employees, so Omar Haddad picks it from the list of services instead of searching for the form itself.
\end{rcnote}
