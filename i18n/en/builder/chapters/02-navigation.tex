\chapter{Apps \& Navigation}
\label{ch:builder-navigation}

\section{What will you set up in this chapter?}
\label{sec:builder-navigation-intro}

Publishing a record type is not enough for the team to use it; members must find it where they expect it. This chapter explains two tools: “Apps \& Navigation”, which builds the app list and its sections, gives an app its identity and places saved views in the menu; and “Work navigation”, which decides what each member sees first, such as the “Due for me” list, the shared context and the dashboard data scope.

Saving differs between the two tools and within each. Ordering and switches in “Apps \& Navigation” stay a draft until “Save changes”, while creating an app or section and saving an identity happen at once. In “Work navigation”, all tabs are saved together with the “Save” button.

\begin{rcnote}[Example: Horizon]
After publishing “Visit requests”, the system administrator adds the “Branch services” app and places a section in it that shows the requests, so Omar Haddad finds them in the menu without searching. Then they turn on “Due for me” so Sarah Al-Khatib finds the requests waiting for her decision in one place.
\end{rcnote}

\section{Pages in this chapter}
\label{sec:builder-navigation-pages}

Start with the first four pages to arrange the menu and prepare the app, then move to “Work navigation” when you want a clearer experience for each role. The two main pages stand on their own in Builder's “Core” section; the other pages explain dialogs or tabs inside them.
